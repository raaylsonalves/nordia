## Peak and layer contract

“It's the site where the pieces of the N connect and the image opens up.”

Title on clean canvas; independently moving two-path vector N in the title; generated material image inside its own frame; stable HTML label/controls outside the image. Image zoom is a bounded transform in natural document flow. N halves converge with separate transforms. No text baked into images. On phones the image has shorter travel. Reduced motion shows the final mark, complete image and all copy without extra scroll space.
